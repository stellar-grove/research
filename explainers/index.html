A Concrete Approach to Mathematical Modelling

Mathematical models you can play with.

Worked, interactive examples for every chapter of Mike Mesterton-Gibbons's book, rebuilt from its topics with our own scenarios and numbers. Each page has the model, the derivation (typeset), a chart, a live slider chart that re-runs the model, and Python using the books.concrete_mathematics module.
