S08 · Smallholder post-harvest loss — hermetic bags, staggered rollout and counterfactual ROI

What is the counterfactual loss reduction and return on investment of hermetic storage bags, estimated from a staggered rollout?

Synthetic case study. Simulated data, not field observations. Seed 260208.

Headline: Hermetic bags cut storage loss ~7 pp (ITT) with a median 3× benefit/cost; a naive TWFE understates the effect by 7% under staggered adoption.

  • Truth recovered 2/2
  • Plausibility checks 3/3

Findings

  • Hermetic bags cut 7-month storage loss by 7.4 pp once households have a season of practice (year-of-adoption effect 6.2 pp), intent-to-treat at 85% uptake.
  • A naive two-way fixed-effects regression reports -6.63 pp vs the event-study average -7.11 pp (truth -7.20) — with staggered, growing effects, already-treated villages contaminate the comparison.
  • Per user household, avoided losses are worth a median $40/season against $13 of amortised bag cost (median benefit/cost 3.0×); 100% of simulated households come out ahead.
