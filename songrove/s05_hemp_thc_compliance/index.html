S05 · Hemp 'hot crop' risk — harvest timing plus lab measurement uncertainty

Given variety, days after floral initiation, sampling date and lab measurement uncertainty, what is the probability a lot tests above 0.3% total THC?

Synthetic case study. Simulated data, not field observations. Seed 260205.

Headline: High-CBD hemp should be sampled by ~day 43 after floral initiation (others by ~day 46) to keep failure risk under 5%; late-season samples fail 29% of the time.

  • Truth recovered 2/2
  • Plausibility checks 3/3

Findings

  • Season failure rate 14%; samples drawn after day 42 (≈October) fail 29% of the time; high-CBD varieties account for 66% of failures.
  • To keep failure risk ≤5%, sample high-CBD lots by day 43 and others by day 46 after floral initiation — 3 days earlier for high-CBD.
  • The 24% measurement-uncertainty allowance matters: without it 33% of lots would fail instead of 14%.
