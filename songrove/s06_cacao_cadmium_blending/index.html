S06 · Cacao cadmium — blending bean lots to meet EU limits without excluding smallholders

Which bean-lot blending plan meets EU cadmium limits for each chocolate category while excluding the fewest smallholders?

Synthetic case study. Simulated data, not field observations. Seed 260206.

Headline: Blending lots to product-level EU limits with 25% headroom buys 100% of demand and cuts unsold lots from 42% to 28%; the planned 15% headroom would fail ~15% of product tests.

  • Truth recovered 2/2
  • Plausibility checks 4/4

Findings

  • The planned 15% headroom is not enough: the weakest blend passes only 85% of product tests. 25% headroom is needed for ≥95% on every blend, at no loss of volume — supply exceeds demand, so the LP simply re-routes high-Cd lots.
  • At 25% headroom, blending buys 470 t of 470 t demand vs 380 t under a per-lot rejection rule; lots left unsold fall from 42% to 28%.
  • In the Piura hotspot, 88% of lots are excluded by the per-lot rule vs 68% with blending: high-Cd beans are diluted into dark >50% and powder instead of being dropped.
