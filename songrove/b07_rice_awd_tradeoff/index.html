B07 · Rice AWD — where does the N2O penalty erode the methane benefit?

Under what soil and climate conditions does the N2O penalty from alternate wetting and drying erode its methane benefit?

Synthetic case study. Simulated data, not field observations. Seed 260107.

Headline: AWD cuts CH4 ~51% while N2O rises ~41%; the N2O penalty is real on acid, warm soils but never erodes the net benefit below a 19% CO2e cut in the modelled range.

  • Truth recovered 6/6
  • Plausibility checks 3/3

Findings

  • N2O's AWD penalty grows +26% per pH unit lower and +24% per 5 °C warmer, but CO2e is dominated by CH4: even on acid, hot soils with a low CH4 baseline the net change is still -19% (a cut).
  • 2% of sites are predicted to get less than a 30% net GWP cut; their mean clay content is 56% vs 32% overall, because clay blunts the CH4 reduction.
