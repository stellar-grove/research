B06 · CO2 nutrient dilution — from meta-analysis to population zinc adequacy

How much does elevated CO2 lower zinc, iron and protein across crops, and what does that do to population-level micronutrient adequacy?

Synthetic case study. Simulated data, not field observations. Seed 260106.

Headline: Elevated CO2 dilutes zinc by ~8% in C3 staples, pushing ~48,000 more people per million below their zinc requirement in a C3-staple population.

  • Truth recovered 8/8
  • Plausibility checks 3/3

Findings

  • Under elevated CO2, C3 crops lose 7.7% zinc, 10.2% iron and 6.6% protein (random-effects pooled; I² for Zn = 0%).
  • C4 crops (maize, sorghum) show 0.21× the C3 dilution, but even with 54 C4 effect sizes the 95% CI (-0.13–0.55) cannot rule out zero or a 50% effect — C4 evidence is the gap to fill.
  • Zinc inadequacy in the synthetic population rises from 36.9% to 41.7% (95% UI 39.9%–43.6%) — about 48,000 more people per million on a C3-staple diet.
