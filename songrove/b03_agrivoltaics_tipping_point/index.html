B03 · Agrivoltaics — the ~2 ha microclimate tipping point and crop-specific shade tolerance

How do panel shading, crop type and system size jointly shape canopy microclimate and yield, and is there a size beyond which the cooling benefit reverses?

Synthetic case study. Simulated data, not field observations. Seed 260103.

Headline: Microclimate benefits reverse beyond ~1.9 ha, and shade tolerance ranges from 10% PAR reduction (maize) to 44% (fruit & berries) for a ≤10% yield penalty — siting should match crop to panel density.

  • Truth recovered 7/7
  • Plausibility checks 3/3

Findings

  • Canopy cooling flattens then reverses beyond ~1.9 ha (95% CI 1.6–2.1 ha); systems larger than ~4 ha run warmer than open field in this simulation.
  • Maximum PAR reduction that keeps yield loss within 10% (normal year): Fruit & berries 44%; Leafy greens 44%; Roots & tubers 22%; C3 cereals 18%; C4 maize 10%.
  • In drought years (SPEI −1.5) each % of shade is worth +0.13 yield points more, so panels act partly as a drought hedge.
