B05 · Maize pollen at anthesis — recovering joint heat and VPD thresholds

What are the Tmax and VPD thresholds for maize pollen viability and spikelet opening, and what does warming do to exposure once VPD is included?

Synthetic case study. Simulated data, not field observations. Seed 260105.

Headline: Recovered thresholds match Guan et al. (viability 32.5 °C / 0.85 kPa); at +2 °C the share of anthesis days losing > 25% viability rises from 43% to 67%, and dry sites carry most of it.

  • Truth recovered 4/4
  • Plausibility checks 4/4

Findings

  • Pollen viability starts falling above 32.5 °C Tmax or 0.85 kPa VPD, losing 5.2 points per °C and 17 points per kPa beyond them.
  • Anthesis days with > 25% viability loss: 43% today vs 67% at +2 °C (same humidity); mean viability loss 24% → 34%.
  • At +2 °C, 60% of dry-site days vs 25% of humid-site days push viability below 60% at similar temperatures — VPD, usually left out of heat-stress models, makes dry sites 2.4× as exposed.
