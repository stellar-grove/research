B04 · Hot nights and rice quality — is night warming worse for chalkiness than day heat?

Does warming concentrated at night damage rice milling and appearance quality (chalkiness, head rice yield) more than daytime heat?

Synthetic case study. Simulated data, not field observations. Seed 260104.

Headline: Night heat is ~2.9× as damaging to rice chalkiness as day heat per °C, and a +2 °C season costs ~6% head rice; a tolerant line halves the night penalty.

  • Truth recovered 6/6
  • Plausibility checks 4/4

Findings

  • Per °C above threshold, night heat raises chalkiness odds 1.32× vs 1.10× for day heat; the night-minus-day difference is 0.18 logit (95% CI 0.04 to 0.32).
  • Without the night-warming treatment the night slope CI is 0.39 logit wide vs 0.14 with it — the manipulation is what separates night from day.
  • A uniform +2 °C season raises chalky grains by 55% (relative) and cuts head rice by 5.6%; night warming alone accounts for 74% of the chalkiness slope.
