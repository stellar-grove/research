S04 · Saffron provenance and adulteration fingerprinting — isotopes, elements and metabolomics together

Can isotope, element and metabolomic features together separate origin, separate harvest year, and detect adulteration?

Synthetic case study. Simulated data, not field observations. Seed 260204.

Headline: Isotopes + elements identify origin (88% cross-validated) while metabolomics tracks harvest year; turmeric and paprika are caught at ~6–9% w/w, safflower only at ~14%.

  • Truth recovered 3/3
  • Plausibility checks 3/3

Findings

  • Cross-validated origin accuracy (5 origins): Metabolomics only 33%; Isotopes only 65%; Isotopes + elements 88%; All features 91%.
  • Metabolites identify the harvest year (46%) better than the origin (33%), matching the finding that metabolic differences are 'not strictly linked to geographical origin'.
  • Detection limits at 95% specificity: turmeric 6.1% w/w; paprika 8.9% w/w; safflower 13.8% w/w. Safflower, with the weakest marker response, slips through below ~14% and needs a more sensitive marker.
