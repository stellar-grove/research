B09 · Microbial inoculants — where in the field do they actually pay?

Why do inoculants that work in the lab disappoint in the field, and which soil covariates predict where they pay?

Synthetic case study. Simulated data, not field observations. Seed 260109.

Headline: Inoculants average 0.7% in the field vs a 15% claim, but the response is predictable from SOM, pH and P: targeting lifts net return from $-24 to $12/ha.

  • Truth recovered 5/5
  • Plausibility checks 3/3

Findings

  • Cross-fitted forest CATEs correlate r = 0.68 with the true site responses without using that farm's own trial; a farm's own strip trial does better (r = 0.78), so the model's value is for farms that have not tested yet.
  • Break-even response is 1.4%. Blanket use nets $-23.7/ha on average; targeting by forecast nets $11.9/ha (oracle $21.3/ha) and treats 40% of farms.
  • The 'efficacy gap': lab claim 15% vs a field average of 0.7% — but low-SOM, near-neutral fields average 6.7%.
