S07 · Vanilla boom-bust — do price spikes cause early harvest and quick curing that lower vanillin?

Do price spikes cause early harvest and quick curing that lower vanillin, and through which channel?

Synthetic case study. Simulated data, not field observations. Seed 260207.

Headline: Price spikes lower vanillin through early harvest and quick curing (-0.54 pts for a +$400/kg boom); early harvest is the larger channel per dollar, quick curing the biggest per-lot penalty (-0.35 pts), and theft pressure adds early picking independent of price.

  • Truth recovered 3/3
  • Plausibility checks 3/3

Findings

  • Each +$100/kg moves vanillin by -0.134 percentage points (95% CI -0.142 to -0.126): -0.074 via earlier harvest, -0.014 via theft-driven early picking, -0.047 via quick curing.
  • At the boom peak, 73% of lots were quick-cured (vs 19% pre-boom) and mean vanillin fell from 2.23% to 1.68%.
  • Theft pressure alone accounts for 4 of the average 19 days early, so village security is a quality lever independent of price.
