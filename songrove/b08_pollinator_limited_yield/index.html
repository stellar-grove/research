B08 · Pollinator-limited yield — which crops and farms would more bee visits actually help?

Which crops and farms are pollination-limited, and how much of the yield deficit would more bee visits close?

Synthetic case study. Simulated data, not field observations. Seed 260108.

Headline: 25/32 crops are pollination-limited; in those, more bee visits could close ~60% of the yield gap to top farms, concentrated in the most pollinator-dependent crops.

  • Truth recovered 5/5
  • Plausibility checks 4/4

Findings

  • 25 of 32 crops are classified pollination-limited (100% agreement with the planted truth; Turo et al. report 25).
  • In limited crops, more visits could close a median 60% of the gap to top-decile farms; the rest is management. 64% of farms per crop underperform.
  • Biggest returns to added visitation at the median farm: buckwheat (+34%), kiwifruit (+34%), cacao (+25%).
