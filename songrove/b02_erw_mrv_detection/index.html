B02 · Enhanced rock weathering — how many soil cores does it take to verify carbon removal?

How many fields and soil cores does it take to verify basalt-driven CO2 removal at a realistic spreading rate?

Synthetic case study. Simulated data, not field observations. Seed 260102.

Headline: At a realistic 20 t/ha dose the removal signal (~0.4 t CO2/ha/yr) is smaller than core-to-core noise; verification is a question of how many fields you sample, not how many cores you take in each.

  • Truth recovered 3/3
  • Plausibility checks 4/4

Findings

  • In the dose trial the slope is significant at 5 cm, 10 cm and not at 30 cm (p = 0.80 at 30 cm), the same pattern McBride et al. report.
  • At 23 t/ha the 5 cm contrast with control is 0.22 t CO2/ha/yr, 95% CI [-2.45, 2.90] — overlapping zero, as in the Scottish trial.
  • Verifying 0.4 t CO2/ha/yr (a 20 t/ha application) with 80% power needs at least 80 paired fields × 10 cores per strip (1600 cores total) under these heterogeneity assumptions.
  • More cores per field saturates fast: going from 10 to 20 cores per strip at 10 fields moves power 24% → 29%; adding fields is the stronger lever because plot-level variation does not average out within a field.
