Buy low, sell high: 2026 hitters whose results outran their contact

Which hitters' 2026 numbers ran ahead of, or behind, how well they actually hit the ball?

Real MLB data: Baseball Savant expected statistics via pybaseball, retrieved 2026-11-20. Regular seasons 2021–2026; hitters with 300+ plate appearances.

Headline: Contact quality is a better guide to next season than results: xwOBA out-predicted actual wOBA in 2 of 3 test seasons. The blend flags J.T. Realmuto as the top buy-low bat (contact quality adds +.021 to his 2027 projection) and Jonny DeLuca as the top sell-high (−.026).

  • 0.53Correlation of xwOBA with next-season wOBA (actual wOBA: 0.47)
  • 4%Less prediction error than results alone, out of sample
  • +.021Top buy-low edge: J.T. Realmuto
  • −.026Top sell-high edge: Jonny DeLuca

Findings

  • Across 994 back-to-back seasons (300+ PA in both), a hitter's xwOBA correlates more strongly with his next-season wOBA (r = 0.53) than his actual wOBA does (r = 0.47).
  • Out of sample, predicting 2023→2024, 2024→2025, 2025→2026 from earlier seasons only, the blend beat last year's wOBA in 2 of 3 seasons and cut error 4% overall (RMSE 28.1 vs 29.1 points of wOBA). Real, but modest.
  • The fitted blend puts about 83% of its weight on xwOBA. Actual results still add a little - sprint speed, spray direction and park, which xwOBA mostly ignores.
  • Next season keeps only about 58% of a hitter's distance from league average, so every projection is pulled toward the middle. Ranking on projected change would just list weak hitters as buys and stars as sells, so the boards rank on the contact edge instead: the part of the projection that comes from xwOBA disagreeing with actual wOBA (0.48 × the gap).
  • The typical miss is still about ±28 points of wOBA, so an edge of ±.020 is a lean, not a lock.

Figures

Method

Projection, with every term measured against that season’s league average w―t (wOBA) or x―t (xwOBA):

wOBAt+1−w―t+1=a+b(wOBAt−w―t)+c(xwOBAt−x―t)

The board ranks on the part of the projection that comes from contact quality disagreeing with results:

contact edge=c[(xwOBAt−x―t)−(wOBAt−w―t)]

with a, b, c fit by PA-weighted least squares on 2021→22 through 2025→26; 2027 assumes the 2026 league level.

  • wOBA weights each way of reaching base by its run value; xwOBA replaces each ball in play with its expected value from exit velocity and launch angle (plus sprint speed on some batted balls), keeping actual strikeouts and walks.
  • The intercept reflects who makes it back: only hitters with 300+ PA in both seasons are in the fit, so projections are conditional on regular playing time in 2027.

Caveats

  • No aging curve, injuries, park changes or lineup context; those belong in a full projection system.
  • xwOBA is blind to spray direction, so extreme pull hitters and shift-ban effects are partly missed.
  • Speed-first hitters routinely beat their xwOBA on infield hits; read their sell-high flags skeptically.
  • Projections are rate stats; playing time is not modelled.
