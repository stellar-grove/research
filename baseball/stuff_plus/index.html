Stuff+: grading pitches on physics, not outcomes

Which pitches are the nastiest on their physical traits alone, and what would make them better?

Real MLB data: Statcast via Baseball Savant. Model trained on 2025 (706,525 pitches), scored out of sample on 2026 (711,248 pitches).

Headline: A pitch's raw traits alone predict a pitcher's future better than his results do: first-half Stuff+ correlates 0.35 with second-half run prevention, vs 0.15 for first-half results. Top pitch of 2026: Tyler Rogers's sinker (Stuff+ 124).

  • 0.35How well first-half Stuff+ predicts the second half (results alone: 0.15)
  • 124Stuff+ of Tyler Rogers's sinker, the top pitch of 2026
  • +0.20Runs saved per 100 pitches from one more inch of four-seam ride

Findings

  • Stuff+ stabilises fast because it's measured on every pitch, while results need hundreds of batters to settle. That makes it the early-season signal for call-ups, trades and slumps.
  • Best arsenal among pitchers with 1,500+ pitches: Jacob Misiorowski (Stuff+ 110).
  • Design lab: on four-seamers, one more mph is worth about +0.12 runs per 100 pitches by the model; see the table for movement and the off-speed pitches.
  • The fastball gap features matter: a changeup or splitter is graded on how different it looks from the same pitcher's fastball, not in isolation.

Figures

Method

Each pitch i is graded on what the pitcher controls, then standardised:

r^i=f(velocityi,spini,movementi,releasei,…),Stuff+i=100+10·μ−r^iσ

where f is the gradient-boosted model of run value fit on 2025 pitches, and μ, σ are the mean and spread of its 2025 predictions (lower run value is better for the pitcher).

  • Target: Statcast delta_run_exp, the change in expected runs on each pitch. Stuff+ is its prediction from traits only, so it ignores location, sequencing and defense.
  • Horizontal break is mirrored for left-handers so arm-side is positive for everyone.
  • Vertical break is Statcast pfx_z (inches of movement relative to a spinless pitch).

Caveats

  • Stuff isn't command: a 130 Stuff+ pitch thrown down the middle still gets hit. A full pitching model would add location (Location+) and sequencing.
  • Trained on one season; a team model would pool several and refit as the league adapts.
