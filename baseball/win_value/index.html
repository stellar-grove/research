The marginal revenue of a win

How much does one more win add at the gate, this season and next?

Real MLB data: MLB Stats API (schedule, attendance, standings), 2022–2026 (150 club-seasons, 12,051 home games). Dollar figures use an assumed $50–$100 of revenue per fan and cover the gate only.

Headline: One extra win brings about 13,672 more home fans this season and 5,131 next season: roughly $1.41M of gate revenue at $75 a fan. That's a fraction of the commonly cited $8–10M free-agent price of a win, so gate alone never justifies a signing; playoff and media revenue have to carry the rest.

Findings

  • Club-season model (same club, better year): +1 win → +13,672 fans this season (95% CI +8,163 to +19,194) and +5,131 next season (+222 to +10,050).
  • Game-level model (season-to-date record): +1 win → +13,520 fans this season (+10,460 to +16,585); carry-over +6,028 (+2,603 to +9,458). The two models agree on the order of magnitude.
  • A playoff berth adds about +213,583 fans over the following season (season total) on top of the wins themselves (95% CI +72,384 to +363,022): season-ticket and plan buyers reward October.
  • Fans respond to winning with a lag: much of a good season's payoff arrives the next year, which argues for valuing contention windows over single seasons.

Method

Club-season model for club c in season t:

lnAttc,tavg=βW%c,t+β′W%c,t−1+λPlayoffsc,t−1+μc+γt+εc,t

One extra win raises win percentage by 1/162, so over 81 home games:

fans per win=(eβ/162−1)×Attlg×81

(and the same with β′ for next season’s carry-over), where Attc,tavg is the club’s average home attendance and Attlg the league average.

  • Club and season fixed effects compare each club with itself and remove league-wide swings.
  • Last season's win% and playoff berth are taken from final standings, so 2021 feeds the 2022 lag.

Caveats

  • Five seasons of 30 clubs is a small panel; the intervals are honest about it.
  • Payroll, new ballparks and star signings move with winning; the fixed effects absorb stable differences but not every change within a club.
  • Attendance is tickets sold (or distributed), not fans through the gate.
